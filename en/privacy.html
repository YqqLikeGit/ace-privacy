Privacy Policy

Updated October 27, 2026

Your Data, Your Choice

Ace Log is made by Yu Quanquan. Your training records and Health calculations stay on your device. Ace Log does not offer account sign-in. We do not sell your data, use advertising SDKs, or track you across other apps and websites.

Health Access

With your permission, Ace Log reads workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, active energy, steps, sleep, and VO₂ max from Health. Optional body metrics include respiratory rate, blood oxygen, and overnight wrist temperature. Cycle records are requested only after you enable Cycle tracking. Date of birth and sex are optional inputs for heart-rate estimates.

Raw Health samples are processed in memory, never uploaded, written to Health, or included in app backups. Turn off individual categories in Health, or disconnect Health in Ace Log. Health-based insights are estimates for training and everyday well-being.

Records, Photos, and Backups

Sessions, matches, opponent names, notes, settings, and chosen photos are stored on this device. Photos are accessed only when you choose them or use the camera. Local backup files include the records and settings you enter, but exclude raw Health samples. Device backups may be managed by your operating system. Any backup or image you export is controlled by you and the destination you choose.

Widgets and Sharing

Widgets use a local shared container for derived summaries and timer state. The timer Live Activity contains no Health metrics. You can hide sensitive widget values. Share images may contain your training information or Health-derived summaries; review the preview before sharing. Nothing is posted automatically.

Optional Message Delivery

The web version can send an activity report through a delivery service you configure. This happens only when you enter a delivery key and explicitly choose to send. The selected report and key are sent to that service; its own privacy policy applies. The key stays in local settings and is excluded from exported backups. Choosing local-only use disables this feature.

Subscriptions

App Store purchases are handled by the platform. Ace Log checks signed purchase records on your device to enable Pro Insights. We do not receive your payment card information. You can restore purchases in Me and manage your subscription in Settings > Subscriptions. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

Delete or Export Your Data

In Me, Export full backup saves a copy of the records you entered. Delete all data removes local sessions, matches, notes, settings, cover photos, app-created backups, widget summaries, and timer state, and ends any active timer. Previously exported copies remain wherever you saved or shared them.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal data. You can withdraw Health access at any time. For privacy requests, contact the developer below. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also contact your local data protection authority.
