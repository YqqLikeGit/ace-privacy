Support

Updated October 22, 2026

Get in Touch

Need a hand with Ace Log? Email yqqwjy@icloud.com. Include your app version, iPhone model, and what you expected to happen. Please avoid sending Health records or other sensitive information unless needed to explain your request.

Connect Health

Open Me and choose Connect Health. Pick the categories you want to share. If a number is missing, check whether Health has recent data for that category. You can keep logging sessions without connecting Health.

Restore Purchases

Open Me and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. To change or cancel a plan, open Settings > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next renewal. A free trial is available only to eligible subscribers.

Keep a Backup

Choose Export full backup in Me before moving devices or deleting data. Import from backup file restores records you entered. Raw Health samples are not included; reconnect Health on the new device.

Delete an Account or Local Data

If you signed in, open Me > Delete account to request account deletion and revoke sign-in access. To clear this device’s records, choose Delete all data. Deleting an account or the app does not cancel a subscription; manage that separately in Settings > Subscriptions.
